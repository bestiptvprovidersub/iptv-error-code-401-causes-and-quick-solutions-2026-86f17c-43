IPTV error code 401: causes and quick solutions 2026

✍️ George Nicholson · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

IPTV error code 401 means authentication failed. Your device could not prove you have a valid subscription.

The fixes below solve this quickly – start with the fastest step.

Common Causes and Quick Fixes (Fastest First)

Cause 1: Expired Subscription → Renew Immediately

The most common reason for error 401 is an expired subscription. Most IPTV services, including premium ones like SubTV IP, require an active plan to stream.

  • Log into your account at your provider’s website.
  • Check your plan end date. If expired, purchase a new subscription.
  • With SubTV IP, activation is instant after payment – no wait time.

Cause 2: Incorrect Login Credentials → Double-Check Details

Typing the wrong username or password triggers error 401. Pew Research Internet surveys show that many users accidentally mistype passwords, especially on TV remotes.

  • Re-enter your credentials slowly. Look for uppercase / lowercase letters.
  • Copy and paste from your confirmation email (useful on Android TV Box or Fire TV Stick).
  • If you changed your password recently, make sure all devices use the new one.

Cause 3: Payment Not Processed → Verify Payment Status

Sometimes a payment goes through on your end but hasn’t cleared on the provider’s side. This can cause a temporary authentication failure.

  • Check your PayPal or credit card statement for the transaction.
  • Contact your provider’s WhatsApp support to confirm the payment is active.
  • SubTV IP uses secure payment options (PayPal and credit card) with instant activation – if payment is successful, error 401 should not appear.

Cause 4: Device Time Is Wrong → Sync with NIST

Many IPTV authentication systems check the device’s clock. If the time is off by more than a few minutes, the server rejects the connection.

  • Go to your device’s date & time settings.
  • Enable “Set automatically” (or “Network time”).
  • For maximum accuracy, sync with NIST time standards (most automatic settings already do this).
  • Restart your IPTV app after changing the time.

Cause 5: Outdated App or Player → Update to Latest Version

Older versions of IPTV players may have authentication bugs that cause error 401. Updates patch these issues.

  • Update TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV to the latest version.
  • Restart the player after the update.

Cause 6: ISP Throttling or DNS Blocking → Use Cloudflare DNS

Some internet providers slow down or block IPTV traffic. This can disrupt the authentication handshake and show error 401.

  • Change your device’s DNS to Cloudflare: 1.1.1.1 (or 1.0.0.1).
  • On Fire TV Stick 4K Max, go to Settings → Network → Advanced → DNS Settings → Manual.
  • On Samsung Smart TV (Tizen) or LG webOS, edit the network connection’s DNS in the advanced menu.
  • This bypasses most ISP throttling. Premium services like SubTV IP use anti-freeze clusters and recommend a 5000 ms buffer for reliable streaming (see next cause).

Cause 7: Incorrect Buffer or Cache Settings → Set to 5000 ms

Many IPTV players allow you to adjust buffer/cache size. If the value is too low, the connection may time out during authentication.

  • Open your IPTV app’s settings (e.g., in TiviMate under “Buffer size”).
  • Set the buffer / cache to 5000 ms (5 seconds).
  • For IPTV Smarters Pro, look for “Advanced Settings” → “Buffer size”.
  • Restart the stream after saving.

What if nothing works?

If you have tried all fixes above and still see error 401, contact your provider’s support team directly.

  • Premium services such as SubTV IP offer WhatsApp support to resolve authentication issues within minutes.
  • They also guarantee 99.9% uptime and instant activation after order, so repeated 401 errors are rare.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does IPTV error code 401 mean?

Error 401 means the server could not verify your subscription. It is an “unauthorized” message – your login or plan is not valid.

Will a VPN fix error 401?

If your ISP blocks IPTV traffic, a VPN can help. However, the cause is often an expired subscription or wrong login. Try the fast fixes first.

How do I know if my IPTV subscription is active?

Log into your provider’s website and check your plan status. With SubTV IP, your dashboard shows the remaining days for 1, 3, or 12-month plans.

Can I fix error 401 by restarting my device?

Sometimes a restart helps if the clock or network was glitched. But if the subscription is truly expired, a restart will not help. Check your plan first.

Why do I get error 401 on one player but not another?

Different apps handle authentication differently. Make sure both players are updated. Also check that you entered the same credentials in each app.

Does SubTV IP offer support for error 401?

Yes. SubTV IP provides WhatsApp support to help you quickly resolve error 401. Their premium subscription includes instant activation and anti-freeze servers.
